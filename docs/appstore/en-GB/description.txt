Bztam ("wallet" in Darija) shows you three things at a glance: what came in, what you spent, and what's left in each category.

ADD EXPENSES IN SECONDS
• Snap a receipt: the amount, shop and date are read on your iPhone.
• Share a screenshot or a PDF bill from any app.
• Type "taxi 20" or just say it.
• Bztam remembers the category you pick for each shop and warns you about duplicates.

BUDGETS THAT FOLLOW YOUR PAYDAY
• Your month starts on your payday, not on the 1st.
• Money left per category, with a pace warning before you overspend.
• Move money between categories for one month.
• A first plan in two minutes: your income, household and bills, and Bztam suggests your budgets. Fixed bills are added by themselves.
• Budget suggestions from your last months.

SAVINGS GOALS
• Save for a trip, Eid or a new phone, with what to put aside each month.
• Money put aside comes off what's left, so it doesn't get spent by accident.

RENT, SALARY AND SUBSCRIPTIONS
• Repeat any entry every week, month or year.
• Bztam spots shops you pay every month and offers to add them automatically.

ASK BZTAM
• "How much did I spend on food last month?"
• "What's left?" "Biggest expense this year?"
• Ask in English, French or Darija. Every number comes from your own entries.
• "Hey Siri, add to Bztam": say "taxi 20" and it's added without opening the app.

SPLIT WITH FRIENDS
• Scan the ticket, pick who was there, split equally.
• Send everyone their share on WhatsApp.
• Counted like your bank: paybacks come back into the category.
• See who owes you and whom you owe, with reminders.

YOUR DART (TONTINE)
• Members in turn order, who paid this turn, whose turn it is to collect.
• Reminders on each turn day, and payments added to your money.

WIDGETS AND ACTION BUTTON
• See what's left on your Home Screen and Lock Screen.
• Add an expense or scan a receipt from Control Center or the Action button.

BZTAM NOTICES FOR YOU
• A weekly recap every Monday.
• An alert when a bill or a category is well above usual.

INSIGHTS AND EXPORT
• Where your money goes, money in vs. out, this period vs. the last.
• Export a PDF report or a CSV for Excel and Numbers.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no bank connection, no ads, no tracking.
• Receipts, voice and questions are processed on your iPhone.
• Privacy mode: one tap hides amounts, or only your money in.
• Optional iCloud sync to your own iCloud. Face ID lock.
• 30 currencies: dirham, euro, dinar, riyal, CFA franc, pound, dollar and more.
• Dark mode, English, French and Arabic.
