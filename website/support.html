Support

Questions, ideas or a receipt Bztam can't read? Write to SUPPORT_EMAIL. We answer in English, French or Darija.

Common questions

When does my month start? On your payday. Change it in Settings → Payday.

A receipt was read wrong. Fix the amount or shop before saving; Bztam remembers the category you pick for that shop.

How do I add a bill from another app? Share it (screenshot, photo or PDF) and choose Bztam, then open Bztam to check and save it.

How do I hide my salary in front of friends? Tap the eye on Home. In Settings → Privacy you can hide amounts every time Bztam opens, or only money in.

How do I add the widget or the Action button? Touch and hold the Home Screen → Edit → Add Widget → Bztam. For the Action button: Settings → Action Button → Controls → Bztam.

Is my data synced? With iCloud sync on (Settings → Data), your data follows your Apple account to your other iPhones. Receipt photos stay on the iPhone that took them.

How do I export? Settings → Data → Export: a PDF report or a CSV for Excel and Numbers.

Ask Bztam doesn't understand me. Try short questions like "spent on food last month". With Apple Intelligence on, it understands more ways of asking.
