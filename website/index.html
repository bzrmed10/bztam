How it works

Three seconds
per expense.

Adding money should never feel like bookkeeping. Bztam reads what you give it and puts it in the right place.

Add

Type it. Say it.
Done.

Write “taxi 20” or tap the mic and say it. Bztam fills the amount and the category, and remembers your choices for next time.

  • Big keypad, one tap per category
  • Speak in English or French
  • Warns you about duplicates
Scan

Receipts, screenshots
and PDF bills.

Snap a receipt, share a screenshot of a phone bill, or open a PDF. The amount, shop and date are read on your iPhone, not on a server.

  • Camera, Photos and Files
  • Share from any app
  • Suggests your newest screenshot
Budgets

Money left,
per category.

Your month starts on your payday. Each category shows what's left, and Bztam warns you when you're spending too fast.

  • A first plan in two minutes
  • Budgets that follow your payday
  • Alerts at 80% and over budget
Insights

See where it
all went.

A monthly recap in plain words, where your money went, money in vs. out, and this period compared with the last.

  • Recap: what's left and what changed
  • Spending by category
  • Budget suggestions from your history

Split with friends

Who owes what,
sorted.

Out with friends and one person pays? Scan the ticket, pick who was there, and Bztam splits it equally and keeps track until everyone has paid back.

  • From a ticket or by handScan the bill or type the total, then choose friends from your own list.
  • Send it on WhatsAppOne tap sends everyone their share.
  • Counted like your bankWhen you pay, the whole bill leaves your money; paybacks come back into the same category.
  • Balances and remindersSee who owes you and whom you owe, and get a reminder after 3 days.
Dart (tontine)

Your dart,
finally organized.

Everyone pays the same each month and one person collects the pot, in turn. Bztam keeps track so nobody has to remember.

  • Members in turn orderTick who paid this turn, see who collects next.
  • Reminders on turn dayA notification at 9:00 on each turn day.
  • Linked to your moneyYour payment becomes an expense, the pot becomes money in.
