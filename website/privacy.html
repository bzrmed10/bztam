Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Bztam does not collect any data. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads, and we never see your money, receipts or questions.

Where your data is

Your entries, categories, budgets, recurring entries and darts are stored on your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud sync (Settings → Data), Apple keeps a copy in your private iCloud database so it appears on your other iPhones. Only you can read it; we cannot. Receipt photos stay on the iPhone that took them.

Reading receipts and questions

Receipts, screenshots, PDF bills, voice input and Ask Bztam questions are processed on your iPhone with Apple's on-device frameworks (Vision, Speech and, when available, Apple Intelligence). Nothing is sent to us or to any other company.

Permissions

  • Camera: to scan receipts.
  • Photos: to look at your newest screenshot and offer to add a bill.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: when you tap the microphone to add an expense or ask a question.
  • Face ID: if you turn on the lock.
  • Notifications: budget alerts and dart reminders, created on your iPhone.

You can turn any of these off in the iPhone Settings app; Bztam keeps working without them.

Exports

CSV and PDF exports are made on your iPhone and only go where you send them.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove its data from your iPhone. To remove the iCloud copy: iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Bztam → Delete Data.

Children

Bztam is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
